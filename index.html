Executive Summary

The Origin

Energy challenges and biomass waste management are deeply connected issues, particularly in coastal regions like Demak, Central Java. In addition to a rising global need for alternative energy to replace fossil fuels, millions of tons of coconut shell waste from the kopra industry go to waste without optimal utilization.

Briket Berkat (NAF Company) was founded to bridge that gap. We productively manage abundant biomass waste, transforming it into charcoal briquettes with high economic value that far exceed the efficiency of conventional charcoal.

Our commitment to preserving the environment is absolute, ensuring future generations can still enjoy the beauty of the Indonesian archipelago.

Market Analysis

Expansion Strategy

Export Market (Primary Focus)

Driven by strong BBQ cultures in Europe (Germany, Netherlands) and social shisha needs in the Middle East (Saudi Arabia, UAE). Export prices promise high margins (USD 1.5 - 2.5/kg).

Domestic Penetration

Routine supply to premium BBQ restaurants, local culinary centers, and catering services in the North Coast of Java (Demak, Kudus, Jepara).

Digital Distribution (Retail)

Retail sales of 1kg, 3kg, and 5kg packs through Shopee, Tokopedia, and aggressive direct marketing via @briketberkat.official.

Competitive Advantage (SWOT)

Strategic Strength: Our production facility in Demak provides a highly efficient logisctics advantage to Tanjung Emas Port, Semarang. This cuts export logistical costs significantly.

Ecosystem Opportunity: Abundant availability of local raw materials (kopra waste) combined with government regulations supporting eco-friendly biomass export.

Scalability Solution: Partnering with the Central Java Briquet Cooperative to accelerate exports and handle NIB & SGS permits more efficiently.

Raw Material Procurement
