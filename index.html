The Origin

Transforming waste into high-value global energy.

Empowering Local Resources

Energy challenges and biomass waste management are deeply interconnected, especially in Central Java. NAF Company was established to bridge this gap.

We productively manage abundant coconut shell waste from local farmers, transforming it into a high-value export product that outperforms conventional energy sources, securing a sustainable future while empowering the local economy.
