Executive Summary

The Origin

Energy challenges and biomass waste management are deeply connected issues, particularly in coastal regions like Demak, Central Java. In addition to a rising global need for alternative energy to replace fossil fuels, millions of tons of coconut shell waste from the kopra industry go to waste without optimal utilization.

Briket Berkat (NAF Company) was founded to bridge that gap. We productively manage abundant biomass waste, transforming it into charcoal briquettes with high economic value that far exceed the efficiency of conventional charcoal.

Our commitment to preserving the environment is absolute, ensuring future generations can still enjoy the beauty of the Indonesian archipelago.

Operational Excellence

6 Manufacturing Stages

01

Raw Material Procurement

Sourcing mature coconut shells with moisture content < 15% from local collectors and Jepara traditional markets.

02

Carbonization (Pyrolysis)

Controlled burning in closed retort kilns (300-500°C) for 4-6 hours. Smoke is recycled for energy efficiency (Zero Waste).

03

Cooling & Milling

Charcoal is cooled for at least 12 hours, then ground using a crusher machine into fine 40-60 mesh powder.

04

Blending (Mixing)

Charcoal powder is homogeneously mixed with a natural tapioca binder liquid (10:1 ratio).

05

Molding

The mixture is processed in a semi-automatic hydraulic press into high-precision shapes (50-80 kg/hour capacity).

06

Drying & Packaging

Thermal ovens reduce moisture content to < 8% over 1-2 days. Followed by strict QC inspection before entering master cartons.
